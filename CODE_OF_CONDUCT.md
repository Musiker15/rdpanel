# Code of conduct

RD Panel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Read it there; the short version for this project is below.

## What we expect

Be kind and assume good intent. Criticize code and ideas, not people. Keep issues, pull
requests and reviews on topic, and remember that many people here write in their second or
third language.

Harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, and publishing someone's private details
without their permission are not accepted anywhere in this project.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, reviews) and wherever someone acts
on behalf of the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer at <bropines@gmail.com>. Say what happened, where (links
help) and who was involved.

The maintainer reviews reports and decides what happens next, guided by the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
